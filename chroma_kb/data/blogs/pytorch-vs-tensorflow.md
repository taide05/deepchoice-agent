# PyTorch vs TensorFlow

**Category**: Models & Data
**Expected winner**: PyTorch

## Analysis

PyTorch has decisively won the research community (80%+ paper implementations use PyTorch). TensorFlow retains some production/edge deployment advantages but for research, PyTorch is the de facto standard.

## Known Contradictions

### Production deployment
- Position A: TensorFlow Serving and TF Lite are more mature for production
- Position B: TorchServe and ONNX Runtime have closed the production gap; PyTorch's research momentum makes it the safer long-term bet
