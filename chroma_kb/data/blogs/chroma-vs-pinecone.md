# Chroma vs Pinecone

**Category**: Models & Data
**Expected winner**: Chroma

## Analysis

Chroma is free, local, open-source. Pinecone is managed, costs money. For solo/small budget, Chroma wins. For scale/production, Pinecone. Budget is the deciding factor here.

## Known Contradictions

### Production readiness
- Position A: Chroma is fine for prototyping but not production-grade at scale
- Position B: Chroma's simplicity is an advantage - fewer moving parts means fewer failures
