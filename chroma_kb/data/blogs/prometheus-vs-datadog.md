# Prometheus vs Datadog

**Category**: Deployment & Operations
**Expected winner**: Prometheus

## Analysis

Prometheus is free and open-source. Datadog charges per host and can cost thousands/month at scale. For a startup on a budget, Prometheus + Grafana is the clear winner. Datadog wins when you can afford the convenience of managed monitoring.

## Known Contradictions

### Total cost of ownership
- Position A: Prometheus is free but requires engineering time to set up and maintain
- Position B: Datadog's monthly cost for a growing startup quickly exceeds the cost of a part-time engineer managing Prometheus
